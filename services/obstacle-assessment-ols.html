AERONAUTICAL IMPACT EVALUATION

Protecting Operational Safety & Aerodrome Viability

GIDEA conducts definitive technical studies to verify whether planned developments compromise current or future air operations.

  • OLS Penetration Analysis: Calculation against Approach, Take-off Climb, Transitional, Inner Horizontal, and Conical surfaces (ICAO Annex 14 Vol I & II).
  • Impact on IFP Minimums: Evaluating if new obstacles increase Obstacle Clearance Altitudes/Heights (OCA/H) or minimum vectoring altitudes (MVA).
  • Wind Farm Impact: Assessing the physical and radar/CNS interference of wind turbine developments in proximity to flight paths.
  • Shielding Principle Applications: Determining if an obstacle is shielded by existing natural terrain or approved permanent structures.
  • Official Authority Reports: Preparing expert technical dossiers required by Civil Aviation Authorities for construction permits.
