Integrated Aerodrome Engineering & Flight Operations

GIDEA ensures that physical infrastructure planning is fully synchronized with instrument flight procedures from day one.

  • Site Selection & Orientation: Wind rose analysis (usable runway coverage > 95%), surrounding terrain clearance, and airspace entry points.
  • Safety Assessments (SMS / ICAO Doc 9859): Hazard identification, risk probability/severity matrices, and operational mitigation strategies for infrastructure modifications.
  • Hospital & Offshore Heliports: Design of PinS procedures, FATO/TLOF sizing, and obstacle environment clearance per ICAO Annex 14 Vol II.
  • Vertiports & Advanced Air Mobility (AAM): Forward-looking operational studies for eVTOL aircraft integration into existing terminal airspace.
