AIRSPACE MODERNIZATION

Terminal Airspace & Route Network Engineering

GIDEA applies advanced Performance-based Navigation (ICAO Doc 9613) and EUROCONTROL airspace design principles to resolve complex congestion.

  • TMA & CTR Restructuring: Re-sectorization and boundary redesign balancing controller workload and traffic throughput.
  • Continuous Descent (CDO) & Climb (CCO): Designing optimum vertical flight paths that reduce engine emissions, fuel consumption, and acoustic impact on neighboring communities.
  • Sector Capacity & Separation: Lateral and longitudinal separation analysis according to ICAO Doc 4444 (PANS-ATM).
  • Transition from Sensor-based to PBN: Decommissioning conventional ground navaids while maintaining required navigation resilience.
