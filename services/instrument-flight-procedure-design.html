COMPREHENSIVE FLIGHT PROCEDURE ENGINEERING

Airports, Aerodromes & Heliports (PinS)

GIDEA LLC delivers robust procedure design uniting mathematical obstacle analysis with flight operational realities.

  • PBN / GNSS Navigation: RNAV 1, RNAV 5, RNP 1, RNP 4, and RNP APCH (LNAV, LNAV/VNAV, LPV).
  • RNP Authorization Required (RNP AR APCH): Advanced curved Radius-to-Fix (RF) legs enabling access to terrain-challenged airports with reduced weather minimums.
  • Conventional Procedures: Non-Precision (VOR, NDB, LOC) and Precision (ILS Cat I, II, III).
  • Terminal Routes: Standard Instrument Departures (SID), Standard Terminal Arrival Routes (STAR), and Missed Approach segments.
  • Heliports & Vertiports (Point-in-Space - PinS): Special instrument procedures for urban, hospital, and offshore helicopter and eVTOL operations.
QUALITY ASSURANCE (QA)

Strict Adherence to ICAO Doc 9906 Quality Process

Every procedure is backed by a complete, auditable Technical Dossier (*Expediente Técnico*) ensuring zero ambiguity and 100% data integrity.

1. Obstacle & Terrain Analysis

Verification of WGS-84 coordinates, geodetic reference surveys, LiDAR obstacle identification, and construction of Obstacle Assessment Surfaces (OAS).

2. Criteria & Safety Verification

Construction of nominal tracks, turn protection areas, climb gradients, and Collision Risk Model (CRM) assessments per PANS-OPS Vol. II.

3. ARINC 424 Coding & Charting

Full navigation database coding sheets (Leg Types, Waypoint descriptors, Altitudes, Speed limits) and official AIP cartography ready for state publication.
